Reading and Writing
1 hour • 50%
7 parts
Part 1 - Multiple choice

Short messages and notices

Read each short notice or message. Choose the answer that shows the main message.

1
Notice: book swap starts at 3.30. Bring your library card. What must students do?
Main message in a notice
2
Message from Mia: I am waiting outside the school library. Where is Mia?
Place words
3
Sign: Please do not eat in the school library. What does the sign mean?
Prohibition signs
4
Text from Leo: The meeting is now in the small room because the old room is too noisy. Why did the place change?
Reason markers
5
Notice: New students should ask Mia for help at the desk. Who helps new students?
Identifying people
6
Message: We cannot use the big room today. Please meet near the office. Where should students meet?
Following changed instructions
Part 2 - Multiple matching

Three short texts on one topic

Read the three texts. For each question, choose Text A, Text B or Text C.

Text A: Mia likes quiet places and wants to practise choosing a book and asking simple questions. Text B: Leo likes working with a group and enjoys making posters for book swap. Text C: The reading club meets at 3.30 and is best for students who can bring a library card.
1
Which text is about a student who likes quiet work?
Matching detail to text
2
Which text mentions making posters?
Finding exact detail
3
Which text tells students to bring a library card?
Object detail
4
Which text is best for choosing a book and asking simple questions?
Purpose matching
5
Which text includes a meeting time?
Time detail
6
Which text is about group work?
People preferences
7
Which text names the reading club?
Club names
Part 3 - Multiple choice

Longer text for detailed understanding

Read the story. Choose the correct answer for each question.

Mia wanted to help at the book swap in the school library. At first, the old room is too noisy, so the teacher asked the students to change the plan. Leo suggested that they should use the small room near the office. The new plan worked well because it is quiet after lunch. By the end of the day, Mia felt proud. The day was busy on Friday but calm on Monday, and everyone learned something useful about choosing a book and asking simple questions.
1
Where did the story happen?
Setting
2
What happened at first?
Sequence words
3
Who suggested the new plan?
Identifying agent
4
Why did the new plan work?
Reasoning
5
How did Mia feel at the end?
Feelings

6

Part 6 - Guided writing

Short email or note

Write 25 words or more. Answer all three points.

1
You are going to the book swap. Write an email to Leo. Say when it is, what to bring, and why you want to go.

Part 7 - Picture story

Story from three prompts

Write 35 words or more. Use the three prompts to make a simple story.

1
Picture prompts: 1) Mia arrives at the school library; 2) the old room is too noisy; 3) use the small room near the office. Write the story.
